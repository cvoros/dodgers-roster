How to play

  1. Pick the 26 players you think make the Dodgers' NLDS roster.
  2. Tap a player to add them. Tap a roster slot to take them off.
  3. Guess the total runs scored in Game 1 (both teams combined) as a tie-breaker.
  4. Press Play Ball when you have 26. Entries are final once confirmed.

Scoring

1 point for each player you get right. Ties go to whoever is closest on Game 1 total runs (over or under), then to whoever entered first. Everyone's picks are revealed when entries lock.
